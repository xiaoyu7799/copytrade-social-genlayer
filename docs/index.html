      Money
      Real testnet GEN on Stable Studionet — free from the faucet, zero cash cost. Each copy stakes exactly 5 GEN (5000000000000000000 wei); each challenge stakes exactly 2 GEN (2000000000000000000 wei). Payouts are withdrawable GEN credits, pulled with Withdraw payout.
      Audit rule
      Five binary checks, exact-match consensus, no score bands. VERIFIED → copy weight 1.0x, SUSPECT → 0.5x, REJECTED → 0x. A record older than 90 days can never be VERIFIED.
      Settlement rule
      Settlement fetches the live BTC price (CoinDesk first, CoinGecko fallback), buckets it to whole $100, and computes PnL = weighted exposure × price-change bps with exact integer division. A winning copy's upside above its own stake is paid from the house pool and capped by it.
